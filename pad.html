Pad Test

Plug in or pair both DualSense controllers, then press a button on each one — Chromium hides a gamepad until it has seen a press while this window has focus. Every pad that has said hello appears below with its live axes, buttons and a rumble test.

On macOS a DualSense usually arrives through Apple's Game Controller framework, which reports 16 buttons and no vendor/product ids — so a row for the PS button or the touchpad simply will not be there. That is normal, and nothing the game binds depends on either.
